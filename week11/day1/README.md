# Data Storytelling — Structure and Narrative

## Objective
To apply data storytelling principles by converting sales data into
meaningful business insights, visualizations, and recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Methodology

1. Created a sample retail sales dataset.
2. Analyzed monthly, regional, and product sales.
3. Identified the highest and lowest performing categories.
4. Created visualizations to communicate important trends.
5. Prepared a business narrative using the following structure:
   - Context: What is happening?
   - Key Findings: What does the data show?
   - Business Challenge: Why does it matter?
   - Recommendation: What should happen next?
   - Conclusion: What is the main takeaway?

## Outputs
- Monthly sales trend chart
- Regional sales comparison
- Product sales comparison
- Business summary CSV
- Business story report

## Key Learning
Data storytelling combines data analysis, visualization, and
communication. It helps stakeholders understand not only what
happened, but also why it matters and what action to take.

## Limitation
The project uses synthetic sales data for demonstration and
learning purposes.
