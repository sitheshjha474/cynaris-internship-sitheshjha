AUTO VIZ - 3 KEY INSIGHTS
=========================

1. Electronics is the highest-selling category with total sales of INR 9,776,377.50.

2. Mumbai has the highest total sales among the cities with INR 2,406,912.60.

3. The Online channel generated higher sales than the Store channel. Online sales were INR 13,167,269.30 compared with INR 9,688,612.48 from Store sales.
