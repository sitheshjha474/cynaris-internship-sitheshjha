BUSINESS STAKEHOLDER RECOMMENDATION
===================================

I would recommend SweetViz for business stakeholders.

The main reason is that SweetViz creates a structured and interactive HTML report that combines visualizations with statistical information. Business stakeholders can review important variables, distributions, comparisons, and relationships without needing to understand Python code.

AutoViz is also useful for analysts because it quickly generates multiple visualizations and helps with exploratory analysis.

Therefore, SweetViz can be used for presenting EDA findings to business stakeholders, while AutoViz can be used by analysts during the initial data exploration stage.
