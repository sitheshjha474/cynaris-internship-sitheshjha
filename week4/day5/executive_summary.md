# Executive Summary

## Business Objective

This analysis evaluates sales and profitability performance for the Kaggle Superstore dataset, with a focus on identifying the effect of discounting, product-level profitability and regional performance. The dataset contains 9,994 retail transaction rows covering 2014–2018.

## Key Findings

- Total sales are approximately **$2.30 million**, with total profit of approximately **$286,397**, giving an overall profit margin of about **12.5%**.
- **Technology** is the strongest category by both sales and total profit, with approximately **$836,154 in sales** and **$145,455 in profit**.
- **Consumer** customers contribute about **50.56% of total sales**, followed by Corporate at about 30.74% and Home Office at about 18.70%.
- Discounting is strongly associated with weaker profitability. Published analyses of this same Kaggle dataset show profitability turning negative once discounts move beyond roughly **20%**.
- Furniture contains important sub-category risks: **Tables and Bookcases** are loss-making areas even though Furniture is a major revenue category.
- Regional performance differs materially, so sales volume should be evaluated together with profit margin rather than used as the only performance measure.

## Recommendations

### 1. Control deep discounting

Introduce stricter approval for discounts above 20% and monitor profit margin by discount band. Promotions should be evaluated on incremental profit rather than sales volume alone.

### 2. Review loss-making products

Conduct a pricing and supplier-cost review for Tables and Bookcases. Products that generate revenue but consistently destroy margin should be repriced, bundled differently, or removed from aggressive promotions.

### 3. Manage regional profitability

Track sales, profit and profit margin together by region and state. Low-margin areas should be investigated for product mix, discounting and operating-cost differences before additional sales investment is made.

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that revenue growth and profitability must be managed together. A combination of discount controls, product-level profitability monitoring and regional performance tracking can help the business protect margins while continuing to grow sales.
